Why you can trust it

Four promises — and where we are

📚 Never guesses the law

If the answer isn't in the official rule books, VAOS says “not found — sent to a reviewer.” It never invents a regulation.

🕒 Always current

It checks whether a rule is still in force, amended (diubah) or revoked (dicabut) — and names the replacement.

🇮🇩 In your language, with proof

Answers in Bahasa Indonesia, quoting the exact pasal word-for-word — so every answer is traceable.

✅ A human approves the action

Nothing is filed as a task until an authorised approver says yes. The AI proposes; a person decides.

Where we are today

The “brain” — understanding the question, finding the rules, deciding, the trust rules — is built and tested. Next: plugging in the live systems (chat, databases, the rule service).
